**QEQ — Quick EQ. Per-app mixer and equalizer for macOS.**

- Subtle system translucency with opaque spectrum plots and light/dark appearance support.
- Cleaner controls, fewer labels, and more room for apps. Options and Quit live beside the master switch.
- Global controls stay visible while editing an app’s EQ, with contained sliding transitions.
- Stable spectrum colors drawn from app icons and a matching monochrome menu bar icon.
- Processing starts enabled by default and remembers your on/off choice.

macOS 14.4+, Apple Silicon and Intel. Quit QEQ before replacing the app; saved settings are retained.
